Sequence:DEV results (Mac, MPS fp32) — official numbers come from the Explorer run

1 · People per frame over time: detections vs ground truth

Grey = GT pedestrians in the frame. Blue = all detections (conf ≥ 0.1). Green = only confident ones (conf ≥ 0.3). The gap between blue and green is what the low threshold buys: weak boxes, mostly partly-hidden people, that ByteTrack's second pass can use.

2 · Recall by how visible the person is (conf ≥ 0.1)

Visibility = fraction of the body not hidden (from MOT20 GT). Occlusion is the main reason people are missed.

3 · The confidence trade-off

Lowering the threshold 0.5 → 0.1 raises recall a lot and costs a little precision. Precision here: boxes on static people/distractors are not counted as errors (TrackEval's rule).

4 · Score distribution of all detections

Most boxes are very confident (right side). The left tail (0.1–0.3) is small but matters for occluded people.
